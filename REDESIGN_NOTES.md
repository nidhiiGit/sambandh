# Sambandh Website Redesign

## What changed
- Rebuilt the homepage around a clearer value proposition for advertising + public relations in North East India.
- Fixed the logo presentation: the full logo is now displayed inside a clean brand card instead of being cropped into a circle.
- Replaced unsupported homepage metrics with claims grounded in the supplied/live website: 20+ years, 5 core services and 3 listed regional offices.
- Added all five services: Public Relations, Advertising, Event Management, News Monitoring, Designing & Printing.
- Added a healthcare-first industry section with the other supplied target sectors.
- Added stronger conversion CTAs and a clearer three-step process.
- Added LocalBusiness + Service JSON-LD to the homepage.
- Updated title, meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata and robots directive.
- Updated navigation, footer and service/about/leadership pages to match the new positioning.
- Updated contact information using the live site's published Siliguri details and listed Guwahati/Agartala offices.

## Important before production
- Verify the phone number, office address, opening hours, named personnel and any client/award claims before publishing.
- Add real client logos, case studies, event photography and press coverage once approved for public use.
- Add unique title/meta descriptions for each route and ideally migrate the SPA to a server-rendered/static architecture for stronger SEO crawling.
- Add `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, Search Console and analytics/conversion tracking.
